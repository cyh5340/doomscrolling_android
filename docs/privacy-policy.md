# Doomscroll Privacy Policy (draft)

_Last updated: [date]. Replace the bracketed parts before publishing, and have it reviewed. This draft describes what
the app actually does, but it is not legal advice._

Doomscroll helps friends keep each other accountable for time spent in social apps. This policy explains what we
collect, what your friends can see, and how to delete it.

## What we collect

- **App usage time, on your phone.** With your permission (Android "usage access"), the app reads how long you spend
  in a fixed list of social apps (for example TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Reddit, X, Snapchat, Facebook). This is
  processed on your phone.
- **What is sent to our server:** your usage rounded down to 15-minute steps per category (for example "social:
  45 minutes on 2026-10-20"), and when a single session passes your group's session limit. Each report includes the
  date, your time zone and the time it happened.
- **Account details:** the display name you choose and an account ID. Accounts are currently anonymous.
- **Groups:** the groups you create or join, their rules, and whether you muted a group.
- **A push notification token** from Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, so we can send friend alerts to your phone.

## What we never collect

- Which specific apps you used.
- Your exact minutes of use.
- Anything you see or do inside other apps.
- Your contacts, location, or advertising ID.

## Who can see your data

- **Members of your groups** see your display name, your time per category in 15-minute steps, your streaks, and
  alerts when you pass the group's limits.
- **Service providers** that run the app for us: Supabase (database and accounts, [region]) and Google Firebase Cloud
  Messaging (delivering notifications). They process data only to provide the service.
- We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access rules on the server let you write only your own data and let group
members read only each other's group data.

## Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data while your account exists. **Delete account** on the app's home screen permanently deletes your
account and all of its data from our server, and clears the app's data on your phone. You can also email
[contact email] to request deletion.

Revoking usage access in Android settings stops tracking immediately.
